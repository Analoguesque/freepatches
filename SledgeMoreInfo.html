Analoguesque Sound Designs - Full bank of 999 Patches
999 Patches that cover every nuance of the Studiologic Sledge's synth engine. Lots of phatt basses, tons of amazing moving pads, searing leads, percussion and drums. This bank uses every asset the Sledge has, to provide a ton of usable sounds, including a bunch of 80's cover sounds, fantastic "analog" drums, and very phat basses, leads and pads. There is no territory this bank does not cover, including FM.

Additionally, this bank contains several "template" patches that use control changed paramteres not available from the front panel of the synth, which brings additional filters, unison modes, filter drive types, noise colors, and the brilliance parameter.
