About Analoguesque Sound Designs

Analoguesque Sound Designs is dedicated to synthesizer programming, electronic music sound design and the exploration of both vintage and modern sound-generation techniques.

The collection brings together sounds and resources for a variety of synthesizers and samplers, with the aim of sharing useful and inspiring sounds with fellow musicians and sound enthusiasts.
