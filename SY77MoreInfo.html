Analog Dreams
64 presets of amazing analog emulations, with a few digital sounds thrown in. Lots of seering analog leads, sweeps, pads, and basses! Many hours were spent perfecting each sound and making sure we took full advantage of what the SY77 synthesis engine has to offer. If you're looking for some analog spice to brighten up your SY77 life, these patches will do it for you!

Digital Vortex
Another 64 presets of grungy vintage patches, with a harder edge and a more defined sound. This bank will appeal to those interested in a little more grittiness to their vintage sounds. More synth leads and super rez sweeps, heavy basses and shimmering pads. As in the other banks, considerable effort was put into these patches to ensure that we maxed out the analog-like features of the SY77. The raw sound of this bank makes some of these patches really phatt.

Some of the pads, leads and basses in this bank are heavy - hard to believe this is a digital synth sometimes, never mind an FM synth.
