Bank 001 - Analog Dreams
This bank has 64 presets of phatt analog sounds to sink your teeth into. Seering leads, thumping basses, mega sweeps, floating pads, and much more. All presets are designed on an Esynth keyboard and take full advantage of all the assignable controllers and sliders, while digging deep into Emu's superior processing power and filters. As in our SoundFont Bank collections, the waveforms in these banks are generated using our proprietary in-house software, so the sounds created are very unique, although retaining a familiar analog feel to them. What's more, they are all in pristine 16-bit, 44.1Khz quality, and digital generation leaves them clear of any distortions due to noise or other sampling anomalies.

These banks can all be saved onto a 3 inch floppy disk and then loaded into your EOS synth. This is the only way you can get the E4B format into your Emu sampler. The E4B format used for the save is compatible with all Emu equipment running the EOS operating system. What is E4B format? It is a DOS-compatible format which is saved from the ESynth onto any DOS-formatted disk. Although extremely slow and agonizing, the E4B format is easy to work with, transfer, and download. It takes some time to load the sounds into your ESynth from floppy, but once they're loaded you can dump them to your ESynth's internal HDD.

Loading the E4B banks into your Emu requires the following:
1. Your Emu must have a working 3 inch floppy drive and be able to accept E4B format files.
2. You must be able to format a DOS floppy.
