# Analoguesque Sound Designs

### Free synthesizer and sampler patches

Welcome to **Analoguesque Sound Designs**, a collection of free
synthesizer patches, sampler sound banks and music-production resources.

This repository is intended as a convenient directory for musicians,
sound designers and synthesizer enthusiasts looking for downloadable
sounds and resources for a range of classic and modern instruments.

Our focus is on creative sound design, distinctive electronic sounds and
making useful resources available to the synthesizer community.

-   Supported instrument or software

## About Analoguesque Sound Designs

Analoguesque Sound Designs is dedicated to synthesizer programming,
electronic music sound design and the exploration of both vintage and
modern sound-generation techniques.

The collection brings together sounds and resources for a variety of
synthesizers and samplers, with the aim of sharing useful and inspiring
sounds with fellow musicians and sound enthusiasts.
